## 🎮 Featured Projects

### ⭐ **Others' Shoes** — [Play on itch.io](https://voidedgamestudio.itch.io/others-shoes)
**A published 3D serious game exploring educational inequality through perspective-shifting gameplay.**

A narrative puzzle game where you embody two university students with identical GPAs and goals, but vastly different life circumstances. Switch between Joe (privileged) and Elyas (working-class) to experience how systemic inequality shapes opportunity — all narrated by a biased, sardonic guide inspired by The Stanley Parable.

- **Core Mechanic:** Perspective-switching reveals how identical objectives require vastly different effort
- **Narrative Design:** Philosophical commentary with dark humour; narrator embodies systemic bias through comedy
- **Technical Depth:** Dual-character system with adaptive gameplay logic, dynamic level design, custom 3D assets
- **Tech Stack:** `Unity 6.3 LTS` • `C#` • `Blender` (3D models/animations)
- **Status:** ✅ **Published & Playable** on itch.io
- **Recognition:** **Final Year Project (FYP)** — Showcases mastery in game design, systems thinking, and narrative design
- **Download Stats:** 16+ downloads, player feedback collected
